# A useful twin predicts the next experiment, not a walking video

## Idea (atomic)

A connectome sim is a **digital twin** iff a held-out prediction changes which split-GAL4 you order. Shiu/Sapkal/Özdil/Pugliese pass that test. Eon’s MuJoCo fly does not: NeuroMechFly already walks without a brain, vision was “decorative,” VNC was skipped, and the 91% figure is Shiu’s unembodied table. Reject “upload.” Keep “rank the next optogenetic line.”

## Why it matters for our harnesses

Same metric as the vault: a note is not learned when it is written ([[permanent/perm-20260911-encoding-beats-exposure]]). A sim is not a fly when it renders. Score models on **held-out biology**, shuffled-connectome controls, and “replace the brain with a 5-line script” ablations.
