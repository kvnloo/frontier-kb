# Score specialists in joules per verified success, not neurons per billion params

## Idea (atomic)

The comparable quantity is **energy (and latency, and money) of the whole deployed loop, including failures and fallback**, divided by externally verified successes. Unknown hosted-model energy stays unknown. Training/teacher cost is a second ledger. A 49k-cell graph with a 50M readout is not a 49k-parameter model. Living-fly microwatts do not transfer to the GPU kernel.

## Why it matters for our harnesses

This is the same conservation law as [[permanent/perm-20260912-physical-computation-is-one-stack]] applied to a product. AODL Kardashev is \(\Gamma_t\) (tokens, attention, joules, spawn), not a new kind. SoL-Pi / ObservationPack work is harness-level joules-per-token; this note is **task-level** joules-per-success. Both can be true without scoring a fly on SWE-Pro.
