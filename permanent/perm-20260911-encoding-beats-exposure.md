# Encoding quality beats exposure time

## Idea (atomic)

A note is not learned when it is written or reread. It is learned when a human groups it against neighbors, names the schema, and can regenerate the claim without the page. Exposure without encoding is noise.

## Why it matters for our harnesses

Agents can dump literature faster than any human can encode. If the frontend shows full markdown first, we train familiarity. humanity-vault must show the distilled claim, force a comparison prompt, then reveal the body.
