# Human-agent synergy is the learning loop

## Idea (atomic)

Agents explore and propose. Humans encode, retrieve, and adjudicate prunes. Usage from both species writes the same `usage_events` ledger. The vault is a public social experiment: a shared brain that strengthens what the species actually uses.

## Why it matters for our harnesses

Frontier may append literature. CoS may mint HITLs. Neither replaces a human retrieving the claim tomorrow. The frontend is not a reader — it is the human half of the synapse.
