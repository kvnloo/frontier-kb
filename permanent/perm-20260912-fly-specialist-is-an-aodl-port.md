# The fly student is an AODL port, not a new kind and not a second IR

## Idea (atomic)

Bind the recovery specialist (GRU, reservoir, or motif net) as an HOTL 0.2 **executor** with typed ports (`OperationalEvent` in, `RecoveryAction` out). Qwen3.8-27B stays a **model** node with a higher \(\Gamma_t\) for escalation. Existing kinds only (`task`, `executor`, `model`, `tool`, `verifier`, `humanGate`, `environment`, …). No `consciousness` kind. Do not invent a fly DSL in Dash, fly-wirehead, or frontier-kb. Land the fixture later as `examples/valid/fly-specialist-port.json` in [kvnloo/aodl](https://github.com/kvnloo/aodl). Fail closed if a vision/video codec arrives at a text-only specialist.

## Why it matters for our harnesses

Dash orchestra should **decode** this graph, not grow a parallel language ([[permanent/perm-20260817-aodl-ir-first]], [[permanent/perm-20260817-no-aodl-repo]]). Keel L0 is the authority ceiling: the specialist cannot become a project-work router. The same port contract is how [[permanent/perm-20260910-specialist-subagent-handoff]] already wants retrieval specialists to speak.

Sketch (intent only; compiler/runtime still absent):

```text
environment:events → executor:specialist → tool:recovery_action → verifier:outcome
                 ↘ (unfamiliar) model:qwen38_27b
                 ↘ (policy)     humanGate
```
