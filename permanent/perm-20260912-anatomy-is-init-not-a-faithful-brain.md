# Once the graph is reorganized, call it fly-inspired

## Idea (atomic)

Anatomy is allowed as **initialization and parameter tying**. After selected synapses, cell-type gains, or connectivity change enough that a retrained rewire is the fair control, the artifact is a **fly-inspired student**, not a simulated fly, not an upload, and not evidence of Drosophila cognition. RigL/Net2Net are the non-biological expand/prune baselines so we can tell whether “growing the connectome” beat ordinary sparse training.

## Why it matters for our harnesses

Stops the naming leak that turned Stonkfly/wirehead/Eon into “the fly learned.” Factory claims should match the object: motif specialist, reservoir readout, or graph policy — each with its control table.
