# Sleep synaptic homeostasis (SHY) as nightly vault decay

## Claim (one sentence)

The synaptic homeostasis hypothesis (Tononi & Cirelli) proposes that sleep globally downscales synaptic weights after a day of potentiation, restoring signal-to-noise — the vault's nightly decay job is that loop.

## Evidence

- SHY (2003 onward): wakefulness nets synaptic potentiation; sleep downscales, improving signal-to-noise and energy cost.
- Huberman's rest-after-learning protocol is compatible: encode while alert, then let rest/sleep do the downscale-and-consolidate pass.

## Fact vs interpretation

- Fact: SHY is a leading (debated) theory of sleep function, not settled law.
- Interpretation: we run exponential decay on idle synapses every night (`half_life_days=14`) and a stronger downscale on unused inbox notes. That is an engineering analog of homeostasis, labeled as such.
