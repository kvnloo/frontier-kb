# Bryan Johnson: measure the loop, don't romanticize effort

## Claim (one sentence)

Protocol plus nightly measurement beats motivation: sleep, consistency, and biomarkers are the control loop; "I tried hard" is not a metric.

## Evidence

- Blueprint / Don't Die public materials treat sleep as the highest-leverage daily intervention and publish measurement dashboards rather than slogans.
- The method we borrow is **closed-loop**: define a biomarker, run a protocol, inspect the number, prune what doesn't move the number.

## Fact vs interpretation

- Fact: Johnson's public stance is that unmeasured self-improvement is mostly narrative.
- Interpretation: this vault's learning biomarkers are retrieval hit-rate, mean synapse weight, idle-days, prune rate, and cycle adherence — not time-on-page. We do not copy his medical stack; we copy the *measurement ethic*.
