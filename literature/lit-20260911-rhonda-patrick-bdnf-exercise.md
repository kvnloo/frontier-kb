# Rhonda Patrick: the brain is metabolic; BDNF is trainable

## Claim (one sentence)

Aerobic and resistance training, omega-3/DHA sufficiency, and related metabolic inputs are publicly discussed as BDNF and cognitive-function levers — so a learning OS that ignores the body is incomplete.

## Evidence

- FoundMyFitness public topic pages discuss BDNF upregulation with vigorous aerobic work, the role of DHA in neuronal membranes, and heat-shock / sauna literature as adjuncts.
- Neighboring science: exercise-induced BDNF and hippocampal plasticity is a large empirical literature. Patrick is the teaching surface we use to keep the vault honest about *physiology as a prerequisite*.

## Fact vs interpretation

- Fact: Patrick's public communication treats brain performance as downstream of sleep, movement, micronutrients, and metabolic health.
- Interpretation: humanity-vault has a MOVE phase before ENCODE. We do not prescribe medical doses. We refuse a "purely cognitive" cram product.
