# SHERWOOD fly-DEX clip is a FlyWire number on an agent-council desk, not a connectome trader

## Claim (one sentence)

[@shmidtqq's 2026-09-12 clip](https://x.com/shmidtqq/status/2098750407770075274) skins a memecoin **agent council** with a decorative fly and the **FlyWire female-brain count** (139,255 neurons / 54.5M synapses); it is not MaleCNS LIF, not Stonkfly's photoreceptor pinout, and not evidence that a biological graph traded a DEX.

## Evidence

Caption: “literal fruit fly” connected into a SHERWOOD terminal; “139,255 sensory neurons”; “12 milliseconds latency”; “1981% pump”; “biological organism is making real DEX trades.”

The ~71 s video (amplify `2098746912740347914`) is a branded **SHERWOOD / ROBINLOW token desk**:

## Fact vs interpretation

- Fact: 139,255 / 54.5M is FlyWire FAFB, already tabulated in [[literature/lit-20260912-optic-lobe-visual-front-end]]. Calling that count “sensory neurons” is false.
- Fact: Stonkfly is an I/O remap of MaleCNS LIF onto a BTC chart with an explicit “profitable learning not established” caveat ([[literature/lit-20260912-fly-connectome-task-meme]]). This clip does not show that kernel.
- Interpretation: this is the trading meme **one layer further from the graph** — a marketing desk with a connectome badge. Digital Sphinx still applies: a PnL GIF is not fidelity ([[literature/lit-20260912-digital-sphinx]]).
- HOLD: no on-chain replay, no neuron IDs, no PAM11/PPL1 current. Do not score Drosophila on memecoin PnL. Do not add a DEX gym to [kvnloo/evolution-lab](https://github.com/kvnloo/evolution-lab). `make_env("sherwood"|"stonkfly")` should fail closed.
